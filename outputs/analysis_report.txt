TASK 15: Operational recommendations
======================================================================
1. Send SMS reminders for every appointment. No-show rate is 27.75% without a reminder versus 15.27% with one, a gap of 12.48 percentage points. Reminders cost little and protect doctor time.
2. Target Dermatology (highest no-show rate, 28.57%) with a second reminder 24 hours before the visit and a simple confirm/cancel reply, so freed slots can be offered to waiting patients.
3. Reduce waiting at Cardiology (average 49.29 minutes). Review how slots are spaced and stagger arrival times; consider extra front-desk or triage support during its peak hours.
4. Add capacity on Monday (average 36.38 appointments per day, the highest of the week) and rebalance load for General Medicine, which has 115.0 appointments per doctor. Move some bookings to quieter days.
5. Give new-patient appointments longer slots, since new patients wait about 7.6 minutes more than follow-ups. Also review the 55 unusually long waits (above 70.0 minutes) to find the doctors and days where delays pile up. Together, 20.4% of attended visits wait over 45 minutes.

Note: This is an operational analysis only. No clinical or medical conclusions are made. Results are based on a synthetic dataset.
